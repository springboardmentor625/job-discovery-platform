Title: SwipeX: Swipe-Based Intelligent Job Discovery and Career Assistance Platform



Objective:



Build a swipe-based intelligent job discovery and career assistance platform that helps candidates discover and apply for relevant jobs using AI-powered resume analysis, ATS scoring, personalized recommendations, and real-time opportunity tracking.



The system should allow users to explore opportunities through an intuitive swipe-based interface, analyze resume compatibility, receive personalized recommendations, track applications, and identify the most suitable opportunities across MNCs, startups, and newly founded companies.



The platform is designed to support modern job discovery by combining artificial

intelligence, recommendation systems, ATS optimization, and career analytics into a centralized application.



This solution can be used by students, fresh graduates, experienced professionals, recruiters, career coaches, and job placement platforms.



Outcomes:



* Developed and deployed a swipe-based intelligent job discovery platform.
* Implemented secure authentication and role-based access control.
* Built swipe-based job exploration and application workflows.
* Integrated AI-powered resume analysis and ATS scoring.
* Developed personalized recommendation and matching systems.
* Implemented real-time job freshness and competition tracking.
* Built dashboards for applications, analytics, and recommendations.
* Added startup discovery and intelligent notification systems.
* Deployed the platform using Docker and cloud deployment platforms such as AWS or Azure.
