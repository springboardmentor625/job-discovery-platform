Database Schema & Relationships:

1. Users
Purpose:
Stores the authentication and basic account information for every user of the platform. It serves as the central table that connects candidates, recruiters, and administrators to the rest of the system.

2. Candidate Profile
Purpose:
Stores the professional information of job seekers, including their education, experience, preferences, and career details used for job recommendations and profile management.

3. Companies
Purpose:
Stores information about companies and organizations that post job opportunities on the platform.

4. Jobs
Purpose:
Stores all job postings along with their requirements, salary details, location, and other information required for job discovery and filtering.

5. Resumes
Purpose:
Stores uploaded resume files and AI-extracted information such as skills. It also supports maintaining multiple resume versions for a candidate.

6. Applications
Purpose:
Tracks every job application submitted by a candidate and maintains its current status throughout the hiring process.

7. Swipe History
Purpose:
Records every swipe action (Like, Dislike, or Save) performed by a user. This data is later used by the recommendation engine to learn user preferences.

8. ATS Reports
Purpose:
Stores the results generated by the AI Resume Analyzer, including ATS scores, resume-job compatibility, missing skills, missing keywords, and improvement suggestions.

9. Recommendations
Purpose:
Stores personalized job recommendations generated by the AI recommendation engine based on resumes, skills, preferences, and swipe history.

10. Notifications
Purpose:
Stores all notifications sent to users, including job alerts, recommendation updates, application status changes, and other platform notifications.

1. Users

user_id (PK), full_name, email (unique), password_hash, role, phone, profile_picture, is_verified, created_at

2. CandidateProfile

profile_id (PK), user_id (FK), headline, summary, location, experience_years, education (JSON/TEXT), projects (JSON/TEXT), certifications (JSON/TEXT), preferred_job_type, preferred_location

3. Companies

company_id (PK), company_name, company_type, industry, website, headquarters

4. Jobs

job_id (PK), company_id (FK), title, description, location, employment_type, salary_min, salary_max, experience_required, required_skills (JSON), posted_date, status

5. Resumes

resume_id (PK), user_id (FK), resume_name, file_path, extracted_skills (JSON), uploaded_at, is_default

6. Applications

application_id (PK), user_id (FK), job_id (FK), resume_id (FK), status, applied_at

7. SwipeHistory

swipe_id (PK), user_id (FK), job_id (FK), swipe_action (LEFT/RIGHT/SAVE), swiped_at

8. ATSReports

ats_report_id (PK), resume_id (FK), job_id (FK), ats_score, match_percentage, missing_skills (JSON), missing_keywords (JSON), suggestions (TEXT), analyzed_at

9. Recommendations

recommendation_id (PK), user_id (FK), job_id (FK), recommendation_score, recommendation_reason, generated_at

10. Notifications

notification_id (PK), user_id (FK), title, message, is_read, created_at
